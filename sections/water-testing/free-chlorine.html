Free Chlorine

Free available chlorine (FAC) by DPD colorimetry on the Hach DR900.

Before sampling

  • Run the tap for 10 minutes before sampling.
  • Test locations must include the farthest distribution point.

You will need

  • DR900
  • Glass sample cell (10 mL)
  • Free chlorine reagent powder pillow (10 mL)
  • Clean cloth/wipe

Procedure

  1. Turn on the DR900. Go to Options and select the chlorine program.
  2. Rinse the sample cell, then fill to the 10 mL line with the water sample.
  3. Wipe the outside with a clean cloth. Insert the cell and place the instrument cover over the sample.
  4. Press ZERO (screen should display 0.00).
  5. Add the DPD Free Chlorine reagent (10 mL pouch) and mix until dissolved.
  6. Wipe the outside with a clean cloth. Insert the sample, place the cover over the sample, and press READ.
  7. Record the result. If < 0.2 ppm, action is required to meet minimum standards.
Record results in the Sick Bay water treatment logbook.
Technical details
  • Local sheet lists Program “80 Chlorine F&T PP” and DPD #8021.
