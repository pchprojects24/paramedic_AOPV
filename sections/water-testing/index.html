Water Testing

Potable water surveillance on AOPV. Routine testing twice weekly at sea; foreign port testing as required; alongside Halifax at least weekly.

Procedures

  • Free Chlorine
  • Total Chlorine
  • pH
  • Bacteriology (Colilert)
Record all results in the Sick Bay water treatment logbook.
